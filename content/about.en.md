The images on this website are digital reproductions of original gelatin silver prints made by the artist on 30.5x40.6cm (12x16in) sheets. Most prints were made in 2018 and onward, although some were made circa 1990. The older prints are unique because of the quality of the emulsions which were then available. Unfortunately emulsions with that quality are no longer produced.

Each print is handmade from the original film negative on baryta-coated fiber-based paper and is signed by the artist.
